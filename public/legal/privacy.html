1. What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, language, password (stored only as a salted Argon2id hash) and, if you use them, your Google or Apple sign-in identifier.
  • Usage: what you watch, how far you got, your list of saved series, notification preferences and simple product events (such as opening the app).
  • Devices and security: sign-in sessions with device name, IP address and time, used to keep your account safe.
  • Payments: plan, amount, status and Razorpay payment references. Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay and never reach us.

2. Why we use it

To create and secure your account, deliver the service and resume where you left off, take and verify payments, send messages you asked for (codes, receipts and, if enabled, new-episode updates), prevent fraud, and improve the product.

3. Who receives it

Only service providers that help us run AIVISTRO: [hosting provider], Razorpay (payments), [SMS provider], [email provider], [push provider] and error monitoring. We do not sell your data.

4. Children

Deleting removes your name, email, phone, date of birth, sign-in methods, devices, sessions, watch history, saved list and notifications.
